Word of the Week

Every week on our Facebook and Instagram pages, we feature a Kalmyk word — presented in both the modern Cyrillic orthography and the traditional todo bichig script — along with its pronunciation and an example phrase/sentence. This page serves as an archive of all past Words of the Week, allowing you to browse, search, and revisit previously featured vocabulary.
